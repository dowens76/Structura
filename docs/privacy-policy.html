Privacy Policy

Structura  ·  Effective date: June 12, 2026

Overview

Structura is a personal Bible study tool. This policy explains how the application handles any information you enter into it.

Personal Information Is Optional

Structura does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the application. Any personal details you choose to enter (such as notes or annotations) are entirely optional and are stored locally on your own device for your personal use.

No Data Transmission

Structura does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with the developer or any third party. All data you enter remains on your device and is never sent over the internet.

No Analytics or Tracking

Structura does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any other third-party tracking tools.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via GitHub.
