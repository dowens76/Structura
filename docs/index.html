Structure your study
of the biblical text.

Source Texts

Standard biblical texts, ready on first launch.

The Hebrew Bible, Septuagint, and Greek NT ship bundled with full morphological tagging. English and Vietnamese translations included. Import any other translation by chapter or full USFM file.

Workspaces

Multiple workspaces with a customizable workbench.

Keep annotations for different projects or passages separate. Customize each workspace's workbench independently, and copy content from one workspace to another.

Display

Three views: clean, color, and interlinear.

Read in a clean unadorned view, switch to color-coded morphology with customizable rules, or open interlinear mode to see lemmas, parsing codes, and more beneath each word.

Markup

Rich markup for every dimension of the text.

Paragraph breaks, indentation, clause relationship arrows, free-form arrows, bold, italics, character tags, speech boxes, word tags, and concept labels — all layered non-destructively on the source text.

Structure

Section breaks and clause labels for discourse structure.

Mark scene and episode breaks across a six-level hierarchy. Annotate lines with plot elements, theme labels (A/B/C), or free-form descriptions — including speech-act classifications.

Presentation

Larger text you can embed in any HTML presentation.

Scale source and translation text for screen sharing or teaching. Embed in a Reveal.js <iframe> with full control over Structura from within the browser.

Notes

Notes per passage, chapter, or verse — with citations.

Write study notes at the passage, chapter, or individual verse level. Include basic citation information from your Zotero account, with configuration.

Reference

Parsing and lexical entries on every word.

Click any word to see full parsing information and a lexical entry — displayed in a tooltip or an expandable sidebar panel, so your reading flow is never interrupted.

Search

Bible search and lookup in a side pane.

Search any passage or look up a cross-reference in a dedicated side pane, powered by fetch.bible or api.bible — without leaving your current workspace.

Import & Export

Import any translation. Export to USFM.

Import Bible translations by chapter with verse numbers, or upload a full USFM file for any translation. A built-in light editor lets you adjust the text. Export back to USFM at any time.

Also included

Additional features

  • Light and Dark modes
  • English and Vietnamese localization
  • Automatic and manual backup with configurable retention
  • Export passages to PDF or PNG
  • Export section breaks to an outline for use in other documents
  • Export notes to MS Word, LibreOffice, or the clipboard
  • And more!
